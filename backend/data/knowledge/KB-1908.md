title: Automated remediation and approvals
category: service_management
access: internal
---
Symptoms: you want to run a runbook on a ticket, or need to know who has to agree to it.

The runbooks are Restart application service, Flush DNS cache, Unlock user account, Rotate service credentials, Promote read replica and Retry mail queue.

Support staff request a runbook from the ticket, giving a target and a justification.

A manager or admin must approve it, and nobody can decide their own request.

The outcome is recorded in the audit log and the requester is told.

In this demonstration the actions are simulated and touch no real system.
