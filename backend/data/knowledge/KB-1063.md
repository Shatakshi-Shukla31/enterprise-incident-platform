---
id: KB-1063
title: Suspicious sign-ins and impossible travel alerts
category: security
access: restricted
team: Security Operations
---
Symptoms: a sign-in from an unexpected country, an impossible travel alert, or an unknown administrator account.

Confirm with the user through a separate channel. If they do not recognise the activity, revoke all sessions and reset their credentials.

For privileged accounts, review every action taken since the first suspicious sign-in, and look for newly created accounts, role grants and application consents.

Rotate any credentials or secrets the account could reach. Rotation runs through the approved runbook.
