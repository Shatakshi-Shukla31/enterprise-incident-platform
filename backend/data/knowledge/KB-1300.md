---
id: KB-1300
title: Lost or stolen laptop or phone
category: hardware
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: a company laptop, phone or tablet is missing, stolen, or was left behind somewhere.

Report it straight away at any hour as a high impact incident, because speed matters more than certainty.

Give the device type, the asset tag if you know it, the last location and time, and whether it was locked.

IT will lock or wipe the device remotely and end its sign-in sessions.

Change your password from another device if the missing one stored any passwords.

For a theft, report it to the police and add the reference number to the incident.
