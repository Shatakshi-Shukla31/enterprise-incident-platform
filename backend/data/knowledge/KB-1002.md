---
id: KB-1002
title: Slow or unreliable office wireless
category: network
access: public
team: Network Operations
---
Symptoms: laptops lose the corporate SSID, have low signal, or crawl in one part of a floor.

Forget the corporate network and rejoin it. Stale profiles cause authentication loops after a certificate renewal.

Turn off any personal hotspot nearby. Phones tethered in meeting rooms interfere with the access points.

Note the room and floor. Network Operations uses the location to find the closest access point. If several people in one area are affected, report it as a single incident and mention how many people.
