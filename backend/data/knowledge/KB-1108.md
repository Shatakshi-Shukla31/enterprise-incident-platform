---
id: KB-1108
title: VPN says connected but internal sites do not open
category: network
access: public
team: Network Operations
---
Symptoms: the VPN client shows connected, yet intranet pages, file shares or internal applications will not load.

Disconnect and reconnect, then try an internal site by its full name.

Restart the laptop if it woke from sleep while connected, because the routes may be stale.

If your home router uses the same address range as the office, the routes collide. Change the home range or report it in an incident.

Turn off any personal VPN running at the same time, because two tunnels compete for the same traffic.
