---
id: KB-1305
title: Webcam or microphone not detected
category: hardware
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: others see a black box instead of your video, the camera is not found, or the microphone is missing in meetings.

Check the privacy shutter on the camera and the camera key on the keyboard.

In Settings, Privacy and security, allow apps to use the camera and microphone.

Close other applications, because usually only one can use the camera at a time.

If you use a dock, plug the device straight into the laptop to rule out the dock.

Reinstall the device from Device Manager if it still does not appear.
