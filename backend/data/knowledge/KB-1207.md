---
id: KB-1207
title: Smart card and certificate sign-in failures
category: access_identity
access: internal
team: Identity & Access
---
Symptoms: smart card sign-in fails, 802.1X network access rejects a device, or a machine certificate is missing or expired.

Check that the certificate has not expired and chains to a trusted certificate authority.

If the certificate revocation list cannot be fetched, sign-in can fail even with a valid card.

Trigger enrolment again with certutil -pulse and confirm the device receives a new certificate.

Check the device clock, because certificates are rejected when the time is wrong.
