---
id: KB-1803
title: Creating or changing a distribution list
category: email_collaboration
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: you want a mailing group for a team or project, its members are wrong, or outsiders cannot send to it.

List owners manage members themselves in the portal.

Request a new list with its name, an owner and a second owner.

Lists accept internal senders only, unless the owner asks for that to change.
