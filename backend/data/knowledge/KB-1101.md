---
id: KB-1101
title: Remote desktop connection to an office computer fails
category: network
access: public
team: Network Operations
---
Symptoms: remote desktop cannot connect, times out, freezes, or rejects your credentials when you reach for your office PC from home.

Connect to the VPN first, because remote desktop is not reachable from the internet.

Use the full computer name rather than a saved address, because office addresses change.

Check that the office computer is switched on and not asleep. Ask a colleague to wake it, or raise an incident for a power check.

If your credentials are rejected, sign in to another work service to confirm your password still works.

Only one person can use a session. Connecting from a second device disconnects the first.
