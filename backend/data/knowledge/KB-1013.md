---
id: KB-1013
title: Application role and permission requests
category: access_identity
access: internal
team: Identity & Access
---
Symptoms: a user needs a new role in a business application, or lost permissions after moving teams.

Team moves trigger an automatic review which removes old roles. This is expected. New access has to be requested through the access catalogue.

Roles that carry financial approval require sign-off from the application owner. Segregation of duties rules prevent one person holding both submitter and approver roles.

Add the approved role to the user's group, then ask them to sign out and back in so that a fresh token picks up the membership.
