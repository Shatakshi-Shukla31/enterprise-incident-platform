---
id: KB-1107
title: Switch port down, VLAN mismatch and err-disabled ports
category: network
access: internal
team: Network Operations
---
Symptoms: one desk or device loses its connection, the link light is off, or a device gets an address from the wrong subnet.

Check the port state and error counters first. A port in err-disabled was shut down by a protection feature, usually port security or BPDU guard.

Find out why it tripped before re-enabling it. A small desk switch or a cable loop brought in by a user is a common cause.

Compare the access VLAN with the documented value for that outlet. An address from the wrong subnet points to a VLAN mismatch.

On a trunk, confirm the VLAN is allowed on both ends. Replace the patch cable before escalating a link that flaps.
