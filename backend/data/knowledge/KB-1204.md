---
id: KB-1204
title: Quarterly access reviews and recertification
category: access_identity
access: internal
team: Identity & Access
---
Symptoms: a manager received a recertification task, or an auditor asks who approved a person's access.

Managers review what each team member can access every quarter.

Remove anything the person no longer needs rather than keeping it just in case.

Reviews left incomplete escalate after 10 working days, and the access is then removed automatically.

The decisions are kept as evidence for audits.
