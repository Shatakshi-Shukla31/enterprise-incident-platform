title: Teams calls, audio and screen sharing problems
category: email_collaboration
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: calls lose audio, screen sharing fails, or chat messages are not delivered.

Check the selected microphone and speaker under device settings, and test with a sample call.

Clear the Teams cache by quitting the app and deleting its cache folder, then restart it. This resolves most message delivery problems.

Screen sharing failures after an update usually need a sign out and back in. On unstable connections, turn off incoming video.
