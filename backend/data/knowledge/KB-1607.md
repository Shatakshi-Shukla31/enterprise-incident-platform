---
id: KB-1607
title: DNS failures inside a Kubernetes cluster
category: cloud_infrastructure
access: internal
team: Cloud Platform
---
Symptoms: pods cannot look up service names, with intermittent could not resolve host errors, or CoreDNS is restarting.

Check the CoreDNS pods, their logs and their restart count.

Test from a debug pod with nslookup kubernetes.default.

For failures under load, add CoreDNS replicas or enable NodeLocal DNSCache.

Network policies must allow UDP and TCP port 53.
