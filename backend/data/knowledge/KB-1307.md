---
id: KB-1307
title: Ordering new or replacement equipment
category: hardware
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: you need a monitor, headset, keyboard or laptop, or your device is due for replacement.

Order through the hardware catalogue in the portal, and your manager approves the request.

Standard items arrive within five working days. Non-standard items need a business reason and budget approval.

Laptops are replaced every four years, or sooner if they are faulty.

Return old equipment to the IT desk so it can be wiped securely.

Buying equipment yourself is not supported, because it cannot be enrolled or secured.
