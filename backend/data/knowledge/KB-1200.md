---
id: KB-1200
title: Accounts and first day sign-in for a new starter
category: access_identity
access: public
team: Identity & Access
---
Symptoms: someone is joining soon, a new joiner has no login, or they cannot sign in on their first day.

Managers raise the new starter request at least five working days before the start date, because accounts are created from the HR record.

On day one the new starter signs in with a temporary password and sets up multi-factor authentication before anything else.

Standard access for the role arrives automatically. Anything extra is requested separately and approved by the application owner.

If the account is missing, check the start date and employee number in the HR record. Accounts are not created until that record is complete.
