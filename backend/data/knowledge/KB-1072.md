id: KB-1072
title: Calendar sync and room booking problems
category: email_collaboration
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: invites do not reach your phone, delegate calendars are not visible, or room booking fails.

Remove and re-add your work account on your phone, as most calendar sync problems come from a stale profile.

For delegate calendars, ask the calendar's owner to check your permissions, then restart Outlook.

If a room shows a free or busy error, try booking from Outlook on the web. If this also fails, report the room's name.
