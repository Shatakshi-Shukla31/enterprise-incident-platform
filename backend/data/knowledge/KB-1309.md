---
id: KB-1309
title: USB storage device blocked by policy
category: hardware
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: a USB stick or external drive will not open, and a message says it is blocked by policy.

USB storage is blocked on company laptops to prevent data loss, so this is expected behaviour.

Use OneDrive or SharePoint to share files instead.

If your role needs removable storage, request an encrypted company drive with a business reason.

Keyboards, mice and headsets are not affected by this block.
