title: SLA targets for each priority
category: service_management
access: public
---
Symptoms: you want to know how long the team has to respond to or fix a ticket, or what an SLA breach means.

P1 tickets get a first response within 15 minutes and a resolution within 4 hours.

P2 tickets get a first response within 30 minutes and a resolution within 8 hours.

P3 tickets get a first response within 4 hours and a resolution within 24 hours.

P4 tickets get a first response within 8 hours and a resolution within 72 hours.

The clock runs through nights and weekends, and it stops only while a ticket is on hold.

An SLA breach means the resolution target was missed. The ticket is escalated one level and its team manager is told.
