id: KB-1903
title: Major incident process
category: service_management
access: internal
---
Symptoms: an outage affects many users or a critical service, and someone needs to run the bridge call.

Declare a major incident for any P1 that affects a critical service.

Appoint an incident manager, who coordinates and leaves the fixing to the engineers.

Open a bridge call and post a status update every 30 minutes.

Restore service first, and find the root cause afterwards.

Hold a blameless review within five working days of closure.
