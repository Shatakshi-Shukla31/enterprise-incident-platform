id: KB-1601
title: ImagePullBackOff and ErrImagePull
category: cloud_infrastructure
access: internal
team: Cloud Platform
---
Symptoms: pods show ImagePullBackOff or ErrImagePull after a deployment, because the container image cannot be fetched.

Check that the image name and tag exist in the registry. A typo or an unpushed tag is the most common cause.

Check imagePullSecrets, because registry credentials expire.

Public registries apply rate limits, so use the internal mirror.

Confirm that nodes can reach the registry through the network rules.
