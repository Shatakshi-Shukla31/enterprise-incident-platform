---
id: KB-1106
title: Guest Wi-Fi for visitors and suppliers
category: network
access: public
team: Network Operations
---
Symptoms: a visitor or supplier needs internet access on their own device, or cannot join the guest network.

Visitors use the guest network, never the corporate wireless network.

Reception issues a voucher that lasts one working day. Hosts can request a longer voucher for visits of several days.

Guest Wi-Fi reaches the internet only, so visitors use their own VPN to reach their own company.

Never plug a visitor laptop into a wired office port.
