title: Problem management and root cause analysis
category: service_management
access: internal
---
Symptoms: the same fault keeps coming back, or you need to find the underlying cause of an outage.

Raise a problem record when an incident repeats three times, and after every major incident.

Build a timeline, then keep asking why until you reach a cause you can act on.

Record any workaround as a known error so others can find it.

Focus on the process rather than on individuals.

Give each corrective action an owner and a date.
