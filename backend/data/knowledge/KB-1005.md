---
id: KB-1005
title: Branch site outage and WAN link failure
category: network
access: internal
team: Network Operations
---
Symptoms: everyone at a site loses connectivity, or there is heavy packet loss on the site's circuit.

Verify power at the site through the local contact, then check the router's status in the monitoring console.

If the primary circuit is down, confirm that traffic has moved to the backup link. If the failover did not trigger, fail over manually using the site's routing runbook.

Open a ticket with the carrier and quote the circuit ID. Treat a whole-site outage as a P1 and post updates every 30 minutes until service returns.
