---
id: KB-1310
title: Laptop not charging or battery drains quickly
category: hardware
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: the laptop is plugged in but the charge percentage does not rise, or the battery runs out within an hour.

Use the charger supplied with the laptop, because a lower wattage charger may not keep up.

Try another wall socket, and on a USB-C laptop try the other port.

Check battery health in the manufacturer's tool. Replacement is free when capacity falls under 60 percent.

If the case is bulging, stop using it and follow the swollen battery article.
