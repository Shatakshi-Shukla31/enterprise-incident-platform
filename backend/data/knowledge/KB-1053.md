---
id: KB-1053
title: Load balancer 503 errors and expired TLS certificates
category: cloud_infrastructure
access: internal
team: Cloud Platform
---
Symptoms: clients get 503 responses, health checks fail behind the ingress, or a browser reports an expired certificate.

A 503 from the load balancer means no healthy targets. Look at the target health reason, and confirm that the health check path still returns 200 after the latest release.

For an expired certificate, check the automatic renewal job and its DNS validation record. Renew the certificate and reload the ingress.

Add certificate expiry monitoring at 30, 14 and 7 days.
