---
id: KB-1808
title: Meeting room display or video system not working
category: email_collaboration
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: the screen in a boardroom stays blank, the touch console is frozen, or there is no sound.

Check that the console is awake and that the screen is on the correct input.

Restart the room system from its settings menu, or power it off and on.

Try the spare HDMI cable if screen sharing fails.

Join from your laptop as a fallback, with its speaker muted to avoid echo.

Report the room name in the incident.
