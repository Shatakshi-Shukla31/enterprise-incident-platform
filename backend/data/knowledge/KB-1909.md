title: Change management and emergency changes
category: service_management
access: internal
---
Symptoms: you need to change production, or a fix is needed during an outage and there is no time for the usual board meeting.

Standard changes are low risk and pre-approved, and they follow a documented procedure.

Normal changes need a risk assessment, a tested rollback plan and peer review before the change board.

An emergency change restores service. The incident manager approves it, and it is documented within two working days.

Avoid changes during freeze periods such as month end.
