---
id: KB-1308
title: Enrolling a phone for work email and apps
category: hardware
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: work apps will not install on a phone, or the company portal app marks it as non-compliant.

Install the company portal app and sign in with your work account.

A compliant phone needs a screen lock, a current operating system and no jailbreak or rooting.

To fix a non-compliant phone, update the operating system, then open the portal app and choose Check status.

On a personal phone, IT manages only the work profile and can only remove work data.
