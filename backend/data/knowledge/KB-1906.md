title: What each notification means
category: service_management

Symptoms: you want to know what an alert means or why you received it.

SLA breached means a ticket passed its resolution target.

High SLA risk means the model estimates a 70 percent or higher chance that the ticket will miss its target.

Response overdue means a ticket has had no first response in time.

Approval needed means a remediation request is waiting for a manager's decision.

Team managers are told about new P1 and P2 tickets, and about alerts on their team's tickets. If a team has no manager, every manager is told.

Each alert is sent once for each ticket. Mark all read clears the unread count and keeps the items.
