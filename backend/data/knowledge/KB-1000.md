The owner of Incident Platform is Shatakshi Shukla.

The support assistant helps with IT incidents using knowledge articles and resolved tickets the signed-in user is allowed to read. Troubleshooting answers cite their sources. If there is not enough evidence, describe the symptoms, the error message, and what you have already tried.

Open Knowledge in the assistant to search for an article. Search by a symptom, such as VPN disconnects, a printer problem, or a password reset. Support managers can add knowledge articles from the main Knowledge page.

Open the + (Add attachments) menu in the chat composer, then choose Image, Audio, Video, PDF, or Files to attach up to three files, each at most 5 MB. The same menu includes Take a photo and Record a voice note. Files remain private to their uploader. The assistant uses text and log attachments to improve its search. Images, audio, video, and PDFs are saved for reference; describe their contents in the message. Voice recording can, where the browser supports speech recognition, add the spoken words to the message.

An OpenAI-compatible provider can generate answers from the retrieved knowledge. Its address, model, and API key are configured on the server. Without a configured provider, the assistant selects and cites sentences from the knowledge base.
