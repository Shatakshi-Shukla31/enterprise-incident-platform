Symptoms: a user reports a credential phishing mail, or submitted credentials on a fake site.

Revoke the user's sessions, reset the password, and require MFA re-registration if a token could be compromised.

Search mail logs for other recipients of the same sender and subject, and purge the message from all mailboxes.

Block the sender domain and the landing page at the mail gateway and the web proxy. Review sign-in logs for the 72 hours after the click for unusual locations and mailbox forwarding rules.
