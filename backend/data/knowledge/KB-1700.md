---
id: KB-1700
title: Sign-in approval requests you did not start
category: security
access: public
team: Security Operations
---
Symptoms: your phone keeps showing MFA or authenticator approval requests that you did not start.

Deny every request. Never approve one just to make it stop.

This usually means someone has your password and is trying to wear you down.

Change your password straight away from a device you trust, then report a security incident.

Include the times of the requests.
