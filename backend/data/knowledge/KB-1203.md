---
id: KB-1203
title: Rotating a service account password
category: access_identity
access: internal
team: Identity & Access
---
Symptoms: an integration fails after a password expired, or a service account is due for a scheduled change.

List every system that uses the account before rotating, because one missed consumer causes an outage.

Where the platform allows it, keep two valid credentials so old and new overlap during the change.

Store the new credential in the vault. Never place it in code, tickets or chat.

Restart consumers in order and confirm each one has authenticated.

Service accounts should never sign in interactively, so investigate any that do.
