---
id: KB-1201
title: Removing access when someone leaves
category: access_identity
access: internal
team: Identity & Access
---
Symptoms: an employee has resigned, a leaver's account is still active, or a manager needs a departing employee's mailbox or files.

Disable the account at the end of the last working day, or immediately for an involuntary exit.

Revoke sessions and remove MFA devices when disabling, because an existing token can outlast a password change.

Reassign shared files, distribution lists and service accounts the person owned before deleting anything.

Keep the mailbox for 30 days, with delegated access for the manager if requested.

Check applications outside single sign-on by hand, because they are the ones most often missed.
