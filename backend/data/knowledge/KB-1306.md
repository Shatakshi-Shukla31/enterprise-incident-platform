---
id: KB-1306
title: Bluetooth headset, keyboard or mouse will not connect
category: hardware
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: a wireless mouse, keyboard or headset will not pair, keeps dropping, or lags.

Remove the device from the Bluetooth list, then pair it again.

Charge the device or fit new batteries, because low power causes dropouts.

Plug a USB receiver into a port away from the dock, because USB 3 ports can interfere with 2.4 GHz signals.

Disconnect a headset from your phone first, as many headsets connect to one device at a time.
