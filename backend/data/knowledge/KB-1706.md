---
id: KB-1706
title: Preserving evidence during a security investigation
category: security
access: restricted
team: Security Operations
---
Symptoms: a device or account is part of an investigation or forensic review, and its contents must be kept intact.

Do not power off, reimage or sign in to the device, because memory contents are lost at shutdown.

Capture memory before disk where possible.

Record who handled each item and when, and hash every disk image, to keep the chain of custody.

Export logs before their retention period ends.
