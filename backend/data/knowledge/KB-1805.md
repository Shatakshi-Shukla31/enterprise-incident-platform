---
id: KB-1805
title: Meeting recording missing or unavailable
category: email_collaboration
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: you cannot find the recording of a Teams call, recording is greyed out, or a recording has expired.

Recordings are saved in the organiser's OneDrive, or in SharePoint for channel meetings.

A recording can take up to an hour to appear in the meeting chat.

Only the organiser and presenters from the same organisation can record.

Recordings expire after 60 days, and the organiser can extend this.
