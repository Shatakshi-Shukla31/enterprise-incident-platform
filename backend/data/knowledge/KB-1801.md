title: Setting an out-of-office reply
category: email_collaboration
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: you need an automatic away message while on leave, or your replies are not being sent.

In Outlook, choose File, then Automatic Replies. On the web, use Settings, Mail, Automatic replies.

Set a start and end time so it turns off by itself.

Replies to people outside the company are written separately on their own tab.

Each sender receives the reply once.
