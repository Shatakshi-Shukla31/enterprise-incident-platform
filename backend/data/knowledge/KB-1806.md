---
id: KB-1806
title: Sharing with external guests in Teams and SharePoint
category: email_collaboration
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: someone from another company cannot join your team, or a sharing link says external sharing is disabled.

A team owner adds the guest by their email address.

The guest accepts the invitation and sets up multi-factor authentication.

Site settings may block guests, so request a change through the portal.

Sites marked confidential are never shared externally.
