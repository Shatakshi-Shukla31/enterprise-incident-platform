id: KB-1109
title: Clock drift and time synchronisation failures
category: network
access: internal
team: Network Operations
---
Symptoms: sign-in fails with a clock skew error, Kerberos tickets are rejected, TLS reports a certificate not yet valid, or logs from two hosts disagree.

Kerberos rejects requests when clocks differ by more than five minutes.

Check the offset on the affected host with w32tm /query /status or chronyc tracking.

Confirm the host can reach its time source on UDP port 123.

Virtual machines can inherit a bad clock from their host, so check both.

Correct the clock once, then find out why it drifted.
