---
id: KB-1703
title: Clicked a suspicious link or opened an attachment
category: security
access: public
team: Security Operations
---
Symptoms: you clicked a link in a strange email or opened an attachment by mistake.

Report it immediately. Reporting quickly limits the damage, and you will not be blamed.

Disconnect from Wi-Fi or unplug the network cable, but leave the computer on so evidence is kept.

If you entered your password, change it from a different device.

Keep the original email, because the security team needs it.
