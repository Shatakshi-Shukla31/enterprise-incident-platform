---
id: KB-1402
title: Software licence activation fails
category: software_application
access: public
team: Application Support
---
Symptoms: an application says its licence has expired, it is unlicensed, or it runs in reduced functionality mode.

Sign in with your work account rather than a personal one.

Connect to the VPN, because licence servers can only be reached internally.

If every seat is in use, request one through the portal.

When you raise an incident, give the product, the version and the exact message.
