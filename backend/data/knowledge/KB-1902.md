id: KB-1902
title: How to write an incident that gets fixed quickly
category: service_management
access: public
---
Symptoms: you are unsure what to put in a ticket, or your tickets keep coming back with questions.

Write a short summary that names the system and the symptom.

Say when it started, what changed, who is affected and what you already tried.

Copy the exact error message, and attach a screenshot if you can.

Raise one ticket for each problem.

Choose impact and urgency honestly, because they set the priority.
