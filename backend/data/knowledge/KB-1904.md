id: KB-1904
title: Escalating an incident
category: service_management
access: public
---
Symptoms: your ticket is stuck, nobody has replied, a deadline is close, or the problem has become more serious.

Add a comment saying what has changed, because that is what support staff see first.

If the impact or urgency has grown, ask for them to be updated, because that changes the priority and the targets.

Support staff can escalate a ticket, which raises its escalation level and notifies the team managers.

Tickets that miss their resolution target are escalated automatically.
