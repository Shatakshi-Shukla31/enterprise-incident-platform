---
id: KB-1060
title: Report a suspicious or phishing email
category: security
access: public
team: Security Operations
---
Symptoms: an unexpected email asks you to confirm a password, open an invoice, or act urgently.

Do not click links, open attachments, or reply. Use the Report Phishing button in Outlook.

If you already clicked a link or entered your password, change your password right away and contact the service desk immediately. Reporting quickly matters far more than having made the mistake.

If several colleagues received the same message, one report is enough, and it will be handled for everyone.
