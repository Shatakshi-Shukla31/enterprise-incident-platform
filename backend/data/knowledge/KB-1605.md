---
id: KB-1605
title: Handling a monitoring alert storm
category: cloud_infrastructure
access: internal
team: Cloud Platform
---
Symptoms: dozens of alerts arrive within minutes and the on-call engineer is paged repeatedly.

Look for the shared cause, such as a dependency, a network fault or a recent deployment, rather than working each alert.

Silence downstream alerts for a fixed period with a reason. Never turn them off permanently.

Open one major incident and link the related alerts to it.

Tune the thresholds afterwards, because every alert should need a human action.
