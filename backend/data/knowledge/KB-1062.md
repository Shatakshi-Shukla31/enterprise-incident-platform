title: Malware and ransomware containment
category: security
access: restricted
team: Security Operations
---
Symptoms: endpoint protection reports a trojan, files are being encrypted, or a ransom note appears.

Isolate the host through the endpoint protection console straight away. Do not power it off, as memory evidence may be needed.

Identify the affected shares and disable the account in use. Check for lateral movement from the host.

Do not restore from backup until the entry point is known and closed. Declare a major incident if a server or multiple hosts are affected.
