---
id: KB-1604
title: Unexpected jump in the cloud bill
category: cloud_infrastructure
access: internal
team: Cloud Platform
---
Symptoms: a budget alert fired, or monthly cloud spend rose sharply.

Open the cost explorer and group by service, then by account and tag, to find what changed and when.

Common causes are forgotten test environments, data transfer charges, log ingestion and unattached volumes.

Stolen credentials can launch expensive instances, so treat unknown regions or GPU instances as a security incident.

Require an owner tag on every resource, and set a budget alert for each team.
