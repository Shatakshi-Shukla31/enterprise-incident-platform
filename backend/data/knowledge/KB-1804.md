title: Email sent to the wrong person
category: email_collaboration
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: you sent a message to the wrong recipient and want to take it back.

Recall only works for internal recipients who have not opened the message. Open it in Sent Items and choose Recall.

If it went outside the company or contains sensitive data, raise a security incident at once, even if the recall seemed to work.

Ask the recipient to delete it and to confirm this in writing.
