Symptoms: a new password is rejected, or you want to know what the password policy requires.

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. A passphrase of several words is easier to remember and stronger than a short complex password.

Your last 10 passwords cannot be reused.

Passwords that appear on known breach lists are rejected even if they meet the length rule.

Use the company password manager for anything not covered by single sign-on.

Support staff will never ask for your password.
