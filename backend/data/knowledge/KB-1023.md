id: KB-1023
title: Swollen battery and overheating laptops
category: hardware
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: the case is bulging, the trackpad lifts, or the laptop overheats and shuts itself down.

A swollen battery is a safety hazard. Stop using the laptop, shut it down, do not charge it, and contact the service desk straight away for a replacement.

For overheating without swelling, make sure the vents are clear and use the laptop on a hard surface. Check Task Manager for a process with sustained high CPU use.

Short battery life on a laptop older than three years is normal wear. A replacement can be requested through the hardware catalogue.
