---
id: KB-1202
title: Privileged and administrator access requests
category: access_identity
access: internal
team: Identity & Access
---
Symptoms: an engineer needs admin rights on a server, database or cloud account, or a request for elevated access was refused.

Privileged access is time-limited. Request it in the access portal with a ticket number and a duration of no more than eight hours.

Standing admin rights are not granted. Use your separate admin account, never your everyday account.

A second person approves each request, and you cannot approve your own.

Every privileged session is logged and reviewed.

Emergency access uses the break-glass account, which alerts Security Operations when used. Its password is changed afterwards.
