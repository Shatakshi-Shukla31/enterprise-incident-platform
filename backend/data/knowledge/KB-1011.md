---
id: KB-1011
title: Account lockout after failed sign-in attempts
category: access_identity
access: internal
team: Identity & Access
---
Symptoms: the user is locked out, often minutes after an unlock, with AADSTS50126 or a similar error.

Check the directory's sign-in log to find the device causing the failures. A phone mail client holding an old password is the most common source.

Unlock the account only after verifying the requester's identity. Have the user update the stored password on each device.

If failures come from an unknown location or an unfamiliar device, do not unlock. Treat it as possible credential compromise and hand over to Security Operations.
