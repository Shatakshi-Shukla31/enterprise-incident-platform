title: How incident priority is worked out
category: service_management
access: public
---
Symptoms: you want to know why a ticket is P1, P2, P3 or P4, or how to choose impact and urgency.

Priority is calculated from impact and urgency, and it cannot be set directly.

Impact is how much of the business is affected. Urgency is how quickly it needs fixing.

High impact with high urgency is P1.

High impact with medium urgency, or medium impact with high urgency, is P2.

Medium impact with medium urgency is P3, and so is high impact with low urgency or low impact with high urgency.

Every other combination is P4.
