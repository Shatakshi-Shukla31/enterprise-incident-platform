---
id: KB-1012
title: Set up multi-factor authentication on a new phone
category: access_identity
access: public
team: Identity & Access
---
Symptoms: authenticator prompts stop arriving after you change phones, or you lost your second factor.

If you still have your old phone, open the security info page and add the new device before removing the old one.

If your old phone is gone, contact the service desk. After verifying your identity they will issue a temporary access pass valid for one hour. Use it to register your new device.

Prompts that arrive late are usually caused by battery optimisation on the phone. Allow the authenticator app to run in the background.
