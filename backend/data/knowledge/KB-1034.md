---
id: KB-1034
title: Report totals do not match or records are duplicated
category: software_application
access: internal
team: Application Support
---
Symptoms: totals disagree with an earlier report, rows are missing, or records are duplicated after a nightly sync.

Find the last successful integration run and compare the record counts at source and destination.

Duplicates after a sync normally mean that a job was retried without an idempotency check. Pause the job before cleaning up, otherwise the problem returns.

Do not edit financial records directly. Corrections have to go through the application so that the audit trail stays intact, and the data owner must approve them.
