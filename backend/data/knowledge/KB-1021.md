---
id: KB-1021
title: Docking station and external monitor problems
category: hardware
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: the dock does not detect monitors, or the keyboard and mouse stop working when docked.

Unplug the dock's power for 30 seconds to reset it, then reconnect it. Most dock faults clear after a power cycle.

Install the latest dock firmware and graphics driver from the software centre. Flickering displays after a Windows update are usually a driver mismatch.

Try another cable, because damaged USB-C cables often carry power but not video.
