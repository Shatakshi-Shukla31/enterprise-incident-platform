---
id: KB-1911
title: AI category and team suggestions on a ticket
category: service_management
access: public
---
Symptoms: a ticket has the wrong category, went to the wrong queue, or you want to know how a team was chosen.

When a ticket is created, one model suggests its category and another recommends a team.

The ticket is routed automatically when the team recommendation is at least 60 percent confident. Below that it waits for manual triage.

Support staff can correct the category, and a manager can move the ticket to the right team.

Corrections are recorded, which shows how often the suggestions are accepted.
