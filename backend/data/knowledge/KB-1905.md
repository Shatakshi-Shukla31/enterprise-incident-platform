title: What incident statuses mean
category: service_management
access: public
---
Symptoms: you want to know what a ticket status means, why a ticket closed, or whether it can be reopened.

New means the ticket is logged and waiting to be picked up. Assigned means someone owns it. In progress means it is being worked on.

On hold means it is waiting for something, and the SLA clock is paused.

Resolved means a fix is in place. A resolved ticket can return to In progress if the fix did not hold.

Resolved tickets close automatically after 7 days.

A closed ticket cannot be reopened, so raise a new one and mention the old number.
