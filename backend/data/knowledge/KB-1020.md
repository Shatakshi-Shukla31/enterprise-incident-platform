---
id: KB-1020
title: Laptop will not power on or shows a black screen
category: hardware
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: the laptop does not start, or the fans run but the screen stays black.

Disconnect the dock and all accessories. Hold the power button for 30 seconds, connect the original charger, and start the laptop again.

If there is sound but no picture, connect an external monitor. If that works, the laptop's display or cable has failed and the device needs repair.

If your work is urgent, ask the service desk for a loan laptop. Your files stored in OneDrive will be available on the loan device.
