---
id: KB-1051
title: Unreachable or unhealthy virtual machines
category: cloud_infrastructure
access: internal
team: Cloud Platform
---
Symptoms: a virtual machine fails status checks, or autoscaling does not replace unhealthy hosts.

Check the instance's status checks and the provider's service health page. A failed system check points to the underlying host, while a failed instance check points to the guest OS.

Stop and start the instance to move it to a new host. A reboot alone does not move it.

If autoscaling is not replacing hosts, check the launch template, quota limits and the health check grace period.
