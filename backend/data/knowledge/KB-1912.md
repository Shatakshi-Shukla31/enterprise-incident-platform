id: KB-1912
title: How to get good answers from the assistant
category: service_management
access: public
---
Symptoms: the assistant says it lacks evidence, or you want to know where its answers come from.

The assistant answers only from knowledge articles and resolved tickets that you are allowed to read.

Every statement links to its source, so you can check it.

If nothing relevant is close enough to your question, it says so rather than guessing.

Describe the symptom in your own words, and include the product and any error message.

The assistant can only give advice. It cannot change anything or take an action for you.
