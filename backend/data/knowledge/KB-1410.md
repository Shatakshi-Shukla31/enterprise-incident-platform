---
id: KB-1410
title: Application signs you out or the session expires too soon
category: software_application
access: public
team: Application Support
---
Symptoms: an application keeps asking you to sign in again, or says your session has expired while you work.

Business applications end a session after 30 minutes without activity.

Allow cookies for the site, because the session is held in a cookie.

Check that your laptop clock is correct, because a wrong time ends sessions early.

If it happens several times an hour while you are active, raise an incident with the times.
