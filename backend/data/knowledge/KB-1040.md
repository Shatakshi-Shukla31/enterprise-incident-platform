---
id: KB-1040
title: Connection pool exhausted and too many clients
category: database
access: internal
team: Database Administration
---
Symptoms: applications report that they cannot connect, SQLSTATE 53300, or too many clients.

Look at active sessions grouped by application. A single service holding most connections points to a connection leak or a stuck release.

Terminate idle-in-transaction sessions older than the agreed limit, then restart the leaking service. Raising max_connections only hides the problem and uses more memory.

For a lasting fix, put a connection pooler in front of the database and set a statement timeout on the application role.
