---
id: KB-1208
title: Windows Hello PIN or fingerprint sign-in not working
category: access_identity
access: public
team: Identity & Access
---
Symptoms: your PIN is not accepted, your fingerprint is not recognised, or Windows says the PIN is unavailable.

Choose Sign-in options on the lock screen and use your password, then reset the PIN in Settings, Accounts, Sign-in options.

Clean the reader and enrol your fingerprint again if it fails repeatedly.

A PIN belongs to one device, so it is not carried over to a new laptop.

After a password change, connect to the office network or VPN so the laptop picks up the new password.
