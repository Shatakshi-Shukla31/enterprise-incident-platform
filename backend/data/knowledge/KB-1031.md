title: Investigating application crashes and unhandled exceptions
category: software_application
access: internal
team: Application Support
---
Symptoms: an application crashes or freezes on a specific action, such as exporting a report.

Reproduce the problem and note the user, the time, and the exact action. Search the application logs for the request at that time to find the stack trace.

Check whether a release went out in the last 24 hours. If the crash began right after a deployment, roll back and raise a defect.

If the crash happens on large exports only, look at memory limits on the worker. Moving the job to a background queue is a longer-term fix.
