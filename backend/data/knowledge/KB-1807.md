title: Recovering a deleted email
category: email_collaboration
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: an email was deleted, or you emptied your Deleted Items folder and need a message back.

Open Deleted Items and choose Recover items deleted from this folder.

Messages can be recovered for 14 days after they leave Deleted Items.

For anything older, raise an incident with the date, sender and subject.
