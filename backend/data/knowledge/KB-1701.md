---
id: KB-1701
title: Payment change requests and invoice fraud
category: security
access: public
team: Security Operations
---
Symptoms: a supplier emails new bank details, or a senior colleague urgently asks for a transfer or gift cards.

Stop and verify by phone, using a number from your own records and not one from the message.

Attackers use lookalike domains, or send from a genuine mailbox they have taken over.

Finance never changes bank details on the strength of an email alone.

If money has already moved, call the bank at once and raise a high impact security incident, because recovery depends on hours.
