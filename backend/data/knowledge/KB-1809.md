---
id: KB-1809
title: Genuine email going to junk, or too much spam
category: email_collaboration
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: expected messages land in your junk folder or quarantine, or you receive more spam than usual.

Check your Junk folder and the quarantine digest, and release messages you trust.

Add a trusted sender to your Safe Senders list.

Avoid unsubscribing from unknown spam, because that confirms your address is active.

If mail from a whole company is blocked, raise an incident and attach the message headers.
