title: What the SLA risk score means
category: service_management
access: public
---
Symptoms: you want to know what a breach probability percentage means, or why a ticket is shown as high risk.

The score estimates the chance that a ticket will miss its resolution target.

It comes from a model trained on simulated tickets, so treat it as guidance.

It uses information known when the ticket opens: category, priority, impact, urgency, channel, team, time and day, description length and team backlog.

Scores of 70 percent or more are high, 40 to 69 percent are medium, and lower scores are low.

P3 tickets raised at a weekend score high because work starts on the next working day while the 24 hour clock keeps running.

Open tickets are rescored every 15 minutes. The score does not change the priority.
