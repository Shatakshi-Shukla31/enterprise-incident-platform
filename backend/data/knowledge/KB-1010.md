---
id: KB-1010
title: Reset a forgotten or expired password
category: access_identity
access: public
team: Identity & Access
---
Symptoms: you forgot your password, or it expired while you were away.

Go to the self-service password portal and choose Forgot Password. You will be asked to verify using your registered phone or authenticator app.

If no code arrives, your recovery details may be out of date. Contact the service desk, who will verify your identity using your employee ID and your manager's name.

After a reset, sign out of your phone and tablet too. Old passwords saved on devices cause repeated failures that lock your account.
