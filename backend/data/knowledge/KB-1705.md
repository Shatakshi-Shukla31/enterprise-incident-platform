---
id: KB-1705
title: Denial of service attack on a public service
category: security
access: restricted
team: Security Operations
---
Symptoms: a public site is flooded with traffic, latency soars, and the load balancer is saturated.

Confirm it is an attack rather than a release or a marketing campaign, using traffic sources and request patterns.

Enable upstream scrubbing and CDN protection before changing anything at the origin.

Apply rate limits and block by request pattern, because single addresses change too quickly.

Open a major incident and keep the logs.
