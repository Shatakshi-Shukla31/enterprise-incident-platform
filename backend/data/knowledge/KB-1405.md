---
id: KB-1405
title: Scheduled job failed or did not run
category: software_application
access: internal
team: Application Support
---
Symptoms: an overnight job did not run, a report is out of date, or the job monitor shows failed or missed.

Check the scheduler to see whether the job started. If it never started, look at the scheduler and the upstream job it depends on.

If it started and failed, read the job log from the first error.

Before running it again, confirm it is safe to repeat, because a rerun can create duplicate records.

Rerun it in a quiet period with the owner's agreement.
