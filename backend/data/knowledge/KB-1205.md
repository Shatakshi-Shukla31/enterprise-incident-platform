---
id: KB-1205
title: Contractor and temporary accounts expiring
category: access_identity
access: public
team: Identity & Access
---
Symptoms: a contractor or vendor login stops working on a particular date, or a warning says the account will expire soon.

Contractor accounts carry an end date that matches the contract, and they expire automatically.

The internal sponsor, not the contractor, extends the date by raising a request with the new contract end date.

Extensions last up to 90 days at a time.
