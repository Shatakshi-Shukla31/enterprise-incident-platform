---
id: KB-1070
title: Mailbox full or email stuck in the outbox
category: email_collaboration
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: you cannot send or receive mail, or a message stays in your outbox.

Empty Deleted Items and archive older messages to free up space. Large attachments use the most quota.

If a message is stuck in the outbox, delete it or move it to Drafts, then send it again without the large attachment. Share a OneDrive link instead.

If a shared mailbox is missing from Outlook, ask its owner to confirm your access, then restart Outlook. It can take up to an hour to appear.
