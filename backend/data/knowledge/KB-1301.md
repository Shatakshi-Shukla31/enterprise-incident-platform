---
id: KB-1301
title: BitLocker recovery key screen at startup
category: hardware
access: public
team: End User Computing
---
Symptoms: the laptop starts on a blue screen asking for a 48 digit recovery key.

This prompt appears after a firmware update, a hardware change or several wrong PINs. It does not mean your data is lost.

Find your key on another device in the company account portal, under your devices.

Support can read the key to you once your identity has been verified.

If the prompt returns at every start, raise an incident so the encryption protectors can be repaired.
